generation's CRC is not evidence about this target. Cold targets and failed

binary semaphore for pure native compute versus drained control/snapshot TX.

authority or durability. Query timing also identifies native debug/hello/
activation intervals numerically, without exposing their payloads.

cache key. Partial redraw retains its previous exact key/bytes when population
is deferred; a later fast draw can populate normally. No additional framebuffer
or relaxed frame budget is used. Unmeasured performance is not acceptance.
